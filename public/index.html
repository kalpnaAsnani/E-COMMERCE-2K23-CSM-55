CAT-04 Variant Combination Integrity Rule

In artisanal ceramics, variant dimensions (e.g. Vessel Size × Glaze Finish) do not form an unchecked Cartesian product. The system represents valid combinations only. An unavailable combination is never created as a fake or zero-stock SKU.
